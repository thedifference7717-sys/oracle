How this record is kept

Every pick is committed to this site's public repository before its game starts, with the time it was published. Nothing is added, edited or removed afterwards: a pick that loses stays on the record.

Each pick is now published sealed at lock: encrypted, next to a fingerprint of it. When its game starts, it is revealed in the clear. Anyone can re-run the fingerprint and find the commit that carried it sealed, which has to be earlier than the game. How to check it yourself.

ROI is at the price each pick was published at (the live Kalshi ask). The Ladder is counted as it is played: each rung at the stake it was published with. The Dub is one unit on each parlay, and the Robin one unit on every ticket of each round robin. Voids and pushes are left out.
