✌️ The Dub

How the Dub works

One parlay a day: the best two-leg parlay across every sport on the slate — baseball hits, basketball points, rebounds, assists and threes, football passing, receiving and rushing yards — each leg priced at its live Kalshi ask.

It is chosen from the same ranked pool as the Ladder, at the same lock, an hour before the day's first game: the two likeliest props between −200 and −350 that our model rates within a few points of their price, from two different games so one game cannot sink both legs, and never the Ladder's own bet — the Dub is a separate bet, not a second helping of the same one.

How much to bet: the first Dub is 10% of the bankroll you set. After a win, the next is 10% of the new balance; after a loss, it is the last bet plus 12.5% ($10, then $11.25, then $12.66…) until a win resets it. A void keeps the same bet.

The legs are close to independent, so the chance both hit is the two chances multiplied. The parlay price shown is what those two Kalshi prices multiply to; your book's parlay price may differ, so type the one you got.

Record

Every Dub is committed to the repo before its first game, so it cannot be improved afterwards. Model estimate — not betting advice. Bet only what you can afford to lose.
