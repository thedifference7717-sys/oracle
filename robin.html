🐦 The Robin

How the Robin works

The six likeliest props across every sport on the slate — baseball hits, basketball points, rebounds, assists and threes, football passing, receiving and rushing yards — bet as a round robin: every 2-leg parlay between them, every 3-leg parlay, and so on up to all six.

A round robin forgives a miss. One cold leg costs only the tickets it is on; with six legs, by 2s is fifteen tickets and a single miss still leaves ten of them alive. The legs come from different games wherever the slate allows, because a round robin only prices honestly when its legs are independent.

Chosen from the same ranked pool as the Ladder and the Dub, at the same lock — between −200 and −350, and only props our model rates within a few points of their price — each leg at its live Kalshi price. How much to bet: the Robin has its own bankroll. Day one is 10% of it; after a day that pays back more than it cost, the next is 10% of the new balance; after a day that doesn't, it is the last bet plus 12.5%. That day's bet is split evenly across the tickets of the size you play. Every row below prices one size at those prices: what each ticket costs, what an average ticket pays, how many should cash, and whether that adds up to more than you put in.
