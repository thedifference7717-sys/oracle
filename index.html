FAQ

Questions, answered.

Do you guarantee winning picks?

No one honestly can. What we guarantee is that every pick is the one our models rate most likely to win, and that every result — win or loss — stays on a public record you can check.

How do I get the picks?

Through a private Telegram channel for your plan. The pick posts the moment it locks, about an hour before the day's first game, along with the price and the stake.

What does "sealed" mean?

Each pick is encrypted and published with a fingerprint and a timestamp before its game, then revealed when the game starts. Anyone can confirm it was posted first.

Which sports are covered?

MLB, NBA, NFL and NHL player props. The Ladder takes the single best pick across all of them.

Can I cancel?

Yes, anytime, from your membership — no questions asked.

What do I need to bet the picks?

Be 21 or older, and an account where player props are legal for you. Prices in the alerts are the live Kalshi ask at lock — here's why we use Kalshi.

What's in each plan?

The Ladder is the day's single top pick. Ladder + Dub adds a daily two-leg parlay. All Access is everything: the Ladder, the Dub, the Robin round robin and all four sport ladders. Each sport ladder (⚾ Strike Zone, 🏈 End Zone, 🏀 Buzzer Beater, 🏒 Top Shelf) is also sold on its own. See pricing.

How does a ladder work?

One pick a day. Start a cycle with your base bet; each win rolls the whole amount onto the next day's pick. Five straight wins completes the cycle and you start fresh. A loss costs only the starting bet, and the next cycle begins a bit bigger to win it back. Every alert tells you the exact stake for that day's rung.

What's a "u" (unit)?

Stakes in the alerts are in units, so they work for any bankroll. 1u is your base bet: if 1u is $10 for you, a 2.5u stake is $25. Pick a unit you'd be comfortable losing.

What time do picks post?

At lock, about an hour before the first game of the day for that pick, once lineups and injury news are in. MLB picks wait for the lineup to post when they can. Times vary with each day's schedule.

What if a player is ruled out?

If news breaks before the game, you get a swap alert with the replacement pick and how to handle a bet you already placed. If a pick can't be graded (the player doesn't play), it's a void: nothing lost, and the ladder stays on the same rung.

What happens when a season ends?

A sport ladder won't start a new cycle without at least five game days left in that sport's season, so you're never stuck mid-cycle. The main Ladder always has a pick, since some sport is always playing.

Is Kalshi available in my state?

Kalshi's sports contracts aren't offered everywhere. Check availability on Kalshi's site before you sign up. You can bet the picks anywhere player props are legal for you; just compare your price to the one in the alert.

I paid. Where's my channel?

Open your membership on Whop (whop.com, signed in with the email you paid with). Your plan's Telegram channel link is there. Connect the same Telegram account you'll read picks on.

Can I get a refund?

Because picks are delivered as soon as you join, payments are non-refundable except where the law requires otherwise. You can cancel anytime, and you keep access until the end of the period you paid for. Full terms.

Can I share the picks?

Paid picks are for you. Please don't repost them before their games start. The free channel @theprop_shop and our posts on X are made to be shared.

I still need help.

Message us through the chat on your Whop membership and a real person will answer. Members can also send /help to the Prop Shop bot (linked in your channel's pinned message) for a quick guide.
