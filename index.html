🪜 The Ladder

How the ladder works

One bet a day, chosen from every sport on the slate: ⚾ a hitter to record a hit, 🏀 a player's points, rebounds, assists or threes, 🏈 a player's passing, receiving or rushing yards. Each sport's model rates every prop, each prop is priced at what it costs on Kalshi, and the ladder takes the one most likely to win — between −200 and −350, and only where the model agrees with the price.

It is not simply the biggest number. A sport's model is trusted only as far as its record has earned: a sport with no settled rungs yet leans on the market's price instead of its own opinion, and earns its say as results come in. Form, the opponent and injury tags break near-ties; a player listed out, or without a real recent workload, is never picked.

Staked from an account you set yourself — every figure on the card is worked out from your number.

Win and the entire return rides the next day. Five straight closes the cycle and the money comes off the table, with the next cycle seeded at 10% of the grown account. Any miss ends the cycle there and the next one starts 25% bigger.

Only the cycle's seed is ever your money — everything above it is the book's — so a miss on day four costs exactly what a miss on day one costs. That is the one thing separating this from a martingale.

The pick stays blank until it locks one hour before the day's first game in any sport, then shows the rung actually published to the repo, with its publish time beside the start. Read the red panel on the card before you run it.

⚙️ Base price

The price a rung is assumed at when neither Kalshi nor you have given one. −275 unless you change it; saved on this device.

⚠ Model estimate — not betting advice. Bet only what you can afford to lose.
