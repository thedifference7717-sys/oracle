No guarantees

No pick is a guarantee, and past results do not predict future results. Our models estimate probabilities; they are wrong some of the time, and you can lose money following them. Model percentages, prices and stakes shown are estimates at the time of publishing. Lines and prices move, and the price you get may differ.

Staking plans such as The Ladder show how a plan has played out on paper. They are examples, not a promise of returns, and you decide what, if anything, to bet.
