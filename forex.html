How this is built
The crypto Fib Ladder for EURUSD, GBPUSD, AUDUSD, USDCHF and USDJPY — same levels, same legs, sized in lots so each pair's ladder loses its share of your stop-loss budget at the stop.

Planning aid only — it places no orders. Every level is derived from the two prices you type, so check the implied swing against your chart before trusting the stop and targets.
