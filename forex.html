The strategy, rule by rule

Best found by tuning (Oct 5) — the crypto Fib Ladder's rules, run on five FX majors.

When it looks for a trade. 4-hour candles, checked as each one closes (6 times a day). A swing high or low needs 20 candles on each side (LuxAlgo Market Structure Breakers, length 20). Both kinds of break trigger, long or short: an MSS (a shift against the prior trend) and an MSB (a break with it). The fib is drawn on the move that made the break — for a long the swing high is 0 and the swing low is 1; shorts are mirrored. While price keeps extending and nothing has filled, the ladder moves to each new high (or low); a newer break replaces an unfilled ladder. Skipped if price has already pulled back past the .5.

Entries. 10 limit orders evenly from the .236 to the .5, sizes ramping so the deepest is 2× the first; orders price has already passed are left out. Everything filled averages about the .38.

Exits. Every leg takes profit at the 0 (the swing high for longs, low for shorts). One stop for the whole position at the 1.272, just beyond where the swing started. When the target fills the unfilled entries are cancelled — the targets share one price, so they fill together (on the engine the stop then goes to break-even and trails for any leg left). No recovery trades. A break the other way does not close a filled trade; it can only replace a ladder that has not filled.

Filters — all must pass.

  1. USD 8h structure (in place of BTC 8h). An equal-weight dollar index built from the five pairs; only trade the side that agrees with its latest 8h break — USD breaking down means longs on EURUSD, GBPUSD, AUDUSD and shorts on USDCHF, USDJPY, and the reverse.
  2. Trend strength. The pair's 4h ADX(14) is 20 or higher at the break.
  3. Funding and top traders — crypto-only (perp funding, Hyperliquid leaderboard). FX has no free equivalent, so they are not applied.

Size. Instead of $250 notional, each ladder is sized so the orders on it, all filled, lose no more than your SL $ per trade at the 1.272 ($400 by default; 5 pairs × $400 = $2,000). Lots round to 0.01 without going over; if the 0.01-lot minimum would overshoot, the shallowest orders are dropped. Because the stop is far (the 1.272) and the target near (the 0), each win is small next to the risk — about 0.4R for a full fill — and the edge is the hit rate.

Placing it on OANDA. The page does not trade. When a ladder goes live, place the listed limits with the shared stop and target; when the ladder moves, move your orders; when the target fills, cancel the rest. Close by hand in OANDA. 4h candles default to OANDA's New York-close timing (bars open 17:00 New York) so the swings match your OANDA chart.

Replay. The record card replays these rules over the candles loaded (≈30 days on Kraken with OANDA timing, ≈4 months on UTC timing, ≈7 months with a Twelve Data key). It fills limits at their price, takes the stop first when a candle spans both, and ignores spread and swap — treat it as a sanity check, not a backtest.

Planning aid only — it places no orders. Confirm every level on your OANDA chart and follow your prop-firm rules.
