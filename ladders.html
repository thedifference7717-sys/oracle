🏆 Sport Ladders

How the sport ladders work

Four more ladders, one sport each, run on the Ladder's rules: one pick a day from that sport alone, priced between −200 and −350, only when our model rates it at or above its price, and a pass on any day nothing qualifies. Each locks an hour before its own sport's first game.

A win rolls the whole return into the next day's pick, five days to a cycle. A miss starts a new cycle from the seed.

Model estimates, not betting advice. Bet only what you can afford to lose.
